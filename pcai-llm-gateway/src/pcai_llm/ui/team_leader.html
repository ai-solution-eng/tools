Team budget envelope

The envelope is a HARD cap on the whole team. Per dimension: Total (or "no cap") · Spent (real usage this period) · Committed (still promised to keys under their allocations) · Available = total − spent − committed — what you may still hand out; the bar fills with spent + committed. Actual spend eats the pool: available, not the old "unallocated", is the number you allocate from.
